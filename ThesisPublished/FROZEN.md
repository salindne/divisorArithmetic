# Thesis source, as published — frozen

> Sebastian Anton Lindner, *Improvements to Divisor Class Arithmetic on
> Hyperelliptic Curves*, PhD thesis, University of Calgary, November 2020.

**Nothing in this directory is ever edited.** It exists so the published work
stays available verbatim while corrections accumulate elsewhere. The other
`README.md` in this directory belongs to the university thesis class and is part
of the frozen snapshot — it is not about this repository.

- **Corrections live in [`../Thesis/`](../Thesis/)**, with every divergence
  listed and justified in [`../Thesis/ERRATA.md`](../Thesis/ERRATA.md).
- The published PDF, [`../ucalgary_2020_lindner_sebastian.pdf`](../ucalgary_2020_lindner_sebastian.pdf),
  is the authoritative artefact of record and is likewise untouched — one commit
  in the repository's history, never modified since.
